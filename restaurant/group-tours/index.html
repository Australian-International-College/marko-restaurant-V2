FOR THE PERSON WITH THE ITINERARY

A smoother plan
starts here.

What group sizes can you accommodate?

Please send your guest count and preferred date. We’ll advise on availability, seating and a suitable arrangement for your group.

Do you offer a group set menu?

Our sharing packages start from A$45 per person, with a starter, sharing dishes, rice, a fruit platter and a drink. Browse Thai Classics, Curry and Comfort, and Grill and Gather, then discuss a menu with us or ask for help choosing. Our team will confirm your menu, price and availability.

Can you plan around our tour schedule?

Include your preferred arrival and departure times when you enquire. We’ll discuss service timing and confirm what is possible for your date.

What about dietary needs and coach arrivals?

Gluten free, vegetarian, nut free and seafood free options are available. Send your group’s dietary requirements, guide contacts and transport plans with your enquiry. Please confirm coach drop-off and parking arrangements separately with your transport provider.
